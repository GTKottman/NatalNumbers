The Equations

Each Sign Is a Sector.
Each Sector Is Math.

Julian Day
JD = 365.25(Y+4716) + 30.6001(M+1) + D + B − 1524.5
Converts your UTC birth date to a continuous astronomical day count.
Sun Mean Longitude
L = 280.460° + 0.9856474° × n
n = days since J2000.0 (JD 2451545.0)
Equation of Center
λ = L + 1.915 sin g + 0.020 sin 2g
Corrects from circular to elliptical orbit, producing the Sun's apparent longitude.
Moon Mean Longitude
L = 218.3164° + 13.176396° × n
Moon moves ~13.2° per day; apparent longitude corrected via ephem.
Mercury Mean Longitude
L = 252.2508° + 4.09233445° × n
Fastest planet; PyEphem resolves apparent geocentric longitude from the orbit.
Venus Mean Longitude
L = 181.9797° + 1.60213034° × n
Inner-planet motion is converted to apparent ecliptic longitude for the report.
Mars Mean Longitude
L = 355.4330° + 0.52402068° × n
Mean orbital motion advances about half a degree per day before correction.
Jupiter Mean Longitude
L = 34.3515° + 0.08308529° × n
Slower outer-planet motion creates long zodiac-sector residence times.
Saturn Mean Longitude
L = 50.0774° + 0.03344414° × n
Small daily motion still maps into the same 30° sector system.
Uranus Mean Longitude
L = 314.0550° + 0.01172834° × n
Long orbital period means sign shifts are measured across years.
Neptune Mean Longitude
L = 304.3487° + 0.00598103° × n
A very small daily rate is refined into apparent longitude by the ephemeris.
Pluto Mean Longitude
L = 238.9290° + 0.00396° × n
Slow generational motion is still reduced to longitude and zodiac sector.
Zodiac Sector
sector = ⌊ λ ÷ 30 ⌋
360° circle ÷ 12 signs = 30° per sector. Floor division maps λ to sign.
Harmonic Ratio
R = a / b = Φ ≈ 1.618
Golden ratio resonance used in vibrational weighting across the chart.
