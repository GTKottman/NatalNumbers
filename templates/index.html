Julian Day
JD = 365.25(Y+4716) + 30.6001(M+1) + D + B − 1524.5

Sun Mean Longitude
L = 280.460° + 0.9856474° × n
n = days since J2000.0 (JD 2451545.0)
Equation of Center
λ = L + 1.915 sin g + 0.020 sin 2g
Corrects from circular to elliptical orbit — the Sun's apparent longitude.
Moon Mean Longitude
L = 218.3164° + 13.176396° × n
Moon moves ~13.2° per day; apparent longitude corrected via ephem.
Zodiac Sector
sector = ⌊ λ ÷ 30 ⌋
360° circle ÷ 12 signs = 30° per sector. Floor division maps λ to sign.
